Tianran Quanli can operate self-sufficiently (self-sustaining), while constructed Quanli requires ongoing maintenance.

3.Consciousness is not part of the real structure; it is an external intervener with completely different characteristics from the real world. It participates in reality in a special way and constructs an entirely new world within reality: human society.
Human society depends on the physical world for existence and, although it operates according to completely different logic, it must be sustained on the basis of Quanli from the physical world.
This structural characteristic determines the conditions for society’s self-sustenance.

4.The Quanli-Information structure of reality can explain and predict the physical world—just as you can know where an apple will fall next; at the same time, it can explain human society and predict the future based on structural facts.
