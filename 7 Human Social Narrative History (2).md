## 宗教之公平世界：心理補償
​在無神顯化的時代，特權的正當性敘事，終究無法完全抹除物理世界中因掠奪與奴役產生的巨大不公感。當人類基因本體對「平權」的渴望在現實痛苦中甦醒，社會動盪便會不可避免地發生。宗教的誕生，正是將這種動盪的心理衝動，精準地導向靈魂的救贖。它以末世的「審判」或來世的「輪迴」等敘事承諾平等，將精神壓抑與物理矛盾，轉化為無法被現實檢驗的個人與神的契約；同時引入施捨、慈悲或禁慾等道德概念，促使特權階級適度釋放閒置資源，作為系統的「安全閥」。

宗教雖然脫離了神在人間的直接統治，卻在神權機制上進行了精巧的修補與應用，構建了一套心理預期系統：
今生的苦難，被重編碼為提升靈魂的「考驗」；
現實的不平等，被解釋為因果的「試煉」；
弱者的犧牲，被許諾為來世的「資產補償」；
特權者的壓迫，則被交付給終極審判去「衡量」。
這使得信眾在心理上獲得了即時的公平感，有效緩解了現實社會的結構矛盾。

在神權與王權仍需依賴武力維持的時代，宗教在人們的意識共識場中構築了一座「心靈監獄」。一旦這座監獄建立，特權便不再完全依賴物理暴力，而是通過因果、罪咎、愛與恐懼的自我流動，實現了權力的自動化運行。這大幅降低了特權階級的系統維護成本，穩定了社會結構，也使得宗教文化得以長盛不衰。當控制規則被內化為信念，權力系統即進入自維持狀態。

在歷史演化中，宗教的公平敘事亦與時俱進。然而，即使在現代法治社會最開明的宗教信眾潛意識中，依然以神的特權為前提，以「神的分配安排」為階級原型來劃分社會。即便他們宣稱尊重良知自由與多元權利，即便他們拒絕任何迫害與報復，也無法掩蓋其「特權團體」的排他邏輯：宗教共同體內部往往以「自身秩序的同構性」作為平等標準；而當這一標準投射到外部社會時，尤其在跨文化碰撞中，便會產生不可避免的結構衝突。例如，戴頭巾的穆斯林女性，在宗教女性群體內部感受到的是「規則內的一致性」公平；但當她們看向外界，其他女性不戴頭巾便成為無法接受的特權行為。因受困於群體信息繭房的邏輯閉環，她們的認知系統缺乏對「系統外文明公平」的識別功能，這使得她們也無法理解自己的行為，會在事實上成為外界人們眼中的特權行為。

任何敘事系統，均以其內部規則的一致性作為公平標準，而非以跨系統的可比性作為判準。當不同敘事系統在同一現實空間中重疊，且彼此無法承認對方的正當性來源時，衝突將不可避免地從認知層滲透至現實層，正如現實世界中廣泛存在的大規模價值觀衝突。這種由「規則內一致性」結構在跨系統接觸中產生的排異，是系統間的結構排異。除非結構毀壞——即價值觀與信仰整體重塑——否則不可調和。在宗教凝聚力衰落的今天，神權邏輯與現代公平制度的碰撞已是正在發生的事實。宗教這種曾經的「穩定器」，已轉化為引爆文明的「炸藥」。正在發生的美以伊戰爭，不是簡單意義上的資源掠奪，更本質的是宗教敘事在物理世界中進行的一場無法回頭的血腥結算。

現代文明中的公平，不再是神的意志秩序，也不再是宗教的心理補償，而是在「法律權利取代神意，法律秩序取代神之秩序」的基礎上，強調個體權利分配的公平。人們相信，公平能在制度設計中實現：人權、法律、民主、契約、自由，這些嶄新敘事共同描繪了一個美麗願景——「每個人都可以被平等對待，擁有相同的基本權利，在相同規則下競爭與生活」。只要所有人在現代民主法律制度的「一致性規則」中互助與競爭，社會就被認定為公平。

然而，人類自以為精巧的這套文明軟體，依然運行在「從屬權力」的底層代碼之上，運行在先驗確定的主從座標系之中。其「一致性的規則」也必然是階級代碼的派生規則，而派生規則無法消除生成它的結構。因此，制度設計只能透過無視個體的物理差異來強行「找平」——無論是形式上的找平、機會上的找平，還是結果上的找平，實質上都只是在調整階級位置或分配順序。這反而必然創造出更隱蔽、更龐大、結構性的階級失衡，即更新形態的不公平。因此，這註定是一場暫時正確、靠延遲結算維持的烏托邦。

神權時代的特權是公開可見的，因為維持神的特權秩序本身即是最大的公平；而在現代，特權關係已成為不公平的表現，於是現代文明將公開「視覺可見」的特權，在民主法治邏輯中改頭換面為「邏輯存在」而「封裝」起來，使其隱匿於資源配置的算法、知識體系的壟斷以及話語權的精微操縱之中。例如，在契約中可以確立從屬關係，掠奪可以在市場競爭中正當化，奴役則藏匿於「資本與結構優勢」之中。隨著科學敘事的日益強大，特權結構也越發隱蔽，這必將導致資源與權力更加集中，也必將導致資源更大規模的閒置。全球超80%的資源被鎖死閒置在極少數階級團體手中，而這些團體卻以引領科技、環保、慈善的「全球監護人」身分現身。可見，現代人類以為自己在進化，事實上只是將「神的寶座」轉移至資本與制度構成的無形黑盒之中（根據World Inequality Report 2026，頂層10%擁有全球財富的75%，底層一半人類僅擁有2%；頂層0.001%擁有比底層一半人類更多的財富（約3倍），资源在符號邏輯上被死鎖）。

在神權時代，如果不公，暴君是可以被挑戰的實體；但在現代黑盒中，人類面對的是冰冷的算法、繁複的條文與宏大的市場，在分散的市場波動、無邊的算法推薦和法條解釋之中根本「找不到實體面對」。在這個「責任主體消失」的荒誕劇場中，個體因找不到受力點而陷入「打在棉花上的絕望」，正是人類集體焦慮與虛無的物理本質。例如全球結構性的低生育率，雖然人們並不明白底層邏輯，但當生命本體在「黑盒」中感受到資源的絕對死鎖，便會以生物性的本能宣告這套代碼的崩潰。

如果人類社會能擺脫「從屬關係」的遺傳基因，放棄先驗確定的階級座標系，那麼，正視不同個體在資源、能力上的實質差異而構造出的公平原則，將是可行的。例如F1賽車設置最低重量限制，它不是在修正特權導致的失衡，而是在無特權狀態下，系統的動態連結與能量的自然流動。

## The Fair World of Modernity: The Truman World

Fairness has always been humanity’s faith; fairness narrative has always been an extremely efficient tool for maintaining order. In the historical process of Quanli transitions, fairness has always been defined and updated by the groups holding discursive power.

Fairness in modern civilization is no longer the order of divine will nor religious psychological compensation, but on the basis of “legal rights replacing divine will and legal order replacing divine order,” it emphasizes fairness in the allocation of individual rights. People believe that fairness can be realized through institutional design: human rights, law, democracy, contracts, freedom — these brand-new narratives jointly paint a beautiful vision: “everyone can be treated equally, possess the same basic rights, and compete and live under the same rules.” As long as everyone engages in mutual aid and competition under the “consistent rules” of modern democratic legal systems, society is deemed fair.

Yet the set of civilized software humanity considers ingenious is still running on the underlying code of “subordinate Quanli,” operating within the a priori determined master-servant coordinate system. Its “consistent rules” are inevitably derivative rules of class code, and derivative rules cannot eliminate the structure that generates them. Therefore, institutional design can only forcibly “level” by ignoring individual physical differences — whether leveling in form, opportunity, or result — in essence merely adjusting class positions or allocation order. This inevitably creates more concealed, larger-scale, structural class imbalances — that is, updated forms of unfairness. Thus, this is destined to be a temporarily correct utopia maintained by deferred settlement.

In the divine Quanli era, privilege was openly visible because maintaining God’s privileged order was itself the greatest fairness. In modernity, privilege relations have become manifestations of unfairness. Thus, modern civilization re-packages openly “visually visible” privilege into “logical existence” under democratic rule-of-law logic and “encapsulates” it, hiding it in algorithms of resource allocation, monopolies of knowledge systems, and subtle manipulations of discursive power. For example, subordinate relationships can be established in contracts, plunder can be legitimized in market competition, and enslavement can be concealed in “capital and structural advantages.” With the growing strength of scientific narrative, privilege structures become increasingly concealed, inevitably leading to greater concentration of resources and power, and inevitably leading to larger-scale idleness of resources. Over 80% of global resources are locked and idled in the hands of a tiny minority of class groups, while these groups present themselves as “global guardians” leading technology, environmental protection, and charity. Clearly, modern humanity believes it is evolving, but in fact it has merely transferred “God’s throne” into an invisible black box composed of capital and institutions (according to the World Inequality Report 2026, the top 10% own 75% of global wealth, the bottom half of humanity owns only 2%; the top 0.001% own more wealth than the bottom half of humanity — approximately 3 times — and resources are deadlocked in symbolic logic).

In the divine Quanli era, if unfair, tyrants were tangible entities that could be challenged. But in the modern black box, humanity faces cold algorithms, complex provisions, and vast markets, so suffering is dispersed into market fluctuations, boundless algorithm recommendations, and legal interpretations with “no tangible entity to confront.” In this absurd theater where “responsible subjects disappear,” individuals fall into “despair punching cotton” because they cannot find a point of force — this is precisely the physical essence of humanity’s collective anxiety and nihilism. For example, global structural low fertility rates — though people do not understand the underlying logic — when life ontology senses the absolute deadlock of resources in the “black box,” it declares the collapse of this code through biological instinct.

If human society could shed the genetic inheritance of “subordinate relationships” and abandon the a priori determined class coordinate system, then fairness principles constructed by confronting the real differences among individuals in resources and capabilities would be feasible. For example, F1 racing sets a minimum weight limit — not to correct imbalances caused by privilege, but to allow dynamic linkage and natural energy flow in a non-privileged state.
