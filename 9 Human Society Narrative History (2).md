相互認可皆在意志互動中發生，因此，意志互動，是社會世界權力關係生成與存在的前提。一旦在意志互動中發生相互認可，即意味著達成了共識，代表了配合將會在物理世界中真實發生，其正是社會世界權力關係本身。
社會權力，都在意志互動的認可中生成，在持續的被認可——信用中延續，信用是社會穩定運轉的必要條件。意識把每次互動都需要重新確認的認可，轉化為制度性信任——法律、契約、貨幣、公司制度等信用體系，都是意識發明出來的，延長認可時效、降低認可成本的工具。
因此，社會權力的存在並不能歸因為強者單方面物理強制的結果，而是所有參與方的共同認可即共識的結果，當任何一方健忘或什麼原因導致無法認可時，對於此方而言，既有社會性權力關係都將被還原成獨立的無配合狀態，便立即導致關於此方的這一社會關係瓦解。換言之，社會性權力關係絕不存在單純的調用、處置，也不存在單向的強制；社會性權力的生成、運行，必須依賴共通的意志——認可或記錄中的認可形成的共識。

Nothing can bypass another's will and directly manipulate another's body. Therefore, cooperation between wills occurs only through conscious recognition. Whether it is voluntary cooperation arising from genuine recognition, passive cooperation driven by fear, submission, habit, or self-interest (physical coercion merely alters the cost of decision-making rather than bypassing the will), or even cooperation performed as deliberate pretense, regardless of the motive, regardless of how deeply distrustful the parties once were, or even if they stand in extreme hostility, the moment cooperation occurs, it is still one's own autonomous will directing one's own body. That moment constitutes an immediate, selective act of recognition. For example, although a victim does not approve of robbery, the victim may nevertheless recognize the act of handing over property as the course of action to be carried out, and it is the victim's own will that moves the body to perform that cooperation. Likewise, if silence ultimately results in observable cooperation, then that silence is likewise regarded as recognition.
Human society did not expand to include strangers and large-scale organizations because trust emerged from cooperation. Quite the opposite: cooperation became possible only after institutional trust had developed beyond personal trust. Institutional trust does not necessarily produce interpersonal trust, yet it does produce cooperation. Likewise, social Quanli is not founded upon universal agreement; it is founded upon the recognition—or acceptance—of wills, that is, upon observable acts of cooperation performed by those wills.
Mutual recognition occurs only through interactions between wills. Accordingly, interaction between wills is the prerequisite for the generation and continued existence of Quanli relationships within the social world. Once mutual recognition arises through such interaction, a consensus has been established, indicating that cooperation will become physically realized. That consensus is itself the social Quanli relationship.
Social Quanli is generated through recognition arising in interactions between wills and endures through continuing recognition—that is, through credit. Credit is an indispensable condition for the stable operation of society. Consciousness transforms the recognition that would otherwise need to be reconfirmed in every interaction into institutional trust. Laws, contracts, money, corporate systems, and other systems of credit are all inventions of consciousness designed to extend the duration of recognition and reduce the cost of obtaining it.
Therefore, the existence of social Quanli cannot be attributed simply to unilateral physical coercion exercised by the strong. Rather, it is the product of the shared recognition—that is, the consensus—of all participating parties. If one party forgets, or for any other reason becomes unable to recognize that relationship, then, for that party, the existing social Quanli relationship immediately reverts to a state of independent non-cooperation, causing that particular social relationship to dissolve. In other words, social Quanli relationships never consist of mere unilateral command, disposal, or coercion. Their generation and operation necessarily depend upon a common will—a consensus formed either through direct recognition or through recognition preserved in records.

任何社會權力關係都僅存在於意志之間，作用於意志指向物理世界而非直接作用於物理世界——在意志互動的認可中發生，在意志互動的配合中顯化為真實，在意志互動的信用中持續；認可、配合、信用共同維持著共識，從而，社會性權力關係便在意志互動中，因失去共識而結束。

All social Quanli relationships exist only between wills. They act upon will and thereby orient action toward the physical world, rather than acting directly upon the physical world itself. They arise through recognition in interactions between wills, become manifest as reality through cooperation between wills, and endure through the credit sustained within those interactions. Recognition, cooperation, and credit together maintain consensus. Consequently, social Quanli relationships persist only so long as that consensus remains, and they come to an end when consensus is lost through interactions between wills.
